## What is the main story or message my visualisations communicate?

The core narrative is: **ICU patients who die within their stay show measurable and consistent physiological deterioration within the first 72 hours — and this signal is detectable from routine clinical measurements.**

The visualisations tell this story across four layers:

### Layer 1 — The Population (Who are these patients?)
- 47,291 ICU admissions, ~12% mortality rate
- Majority aged 55–75, with non-survivors tending to be older
- Care units differ significantly in mortality rates — Coronary Care and Neuro SICU have different risk profiles than general MICU

### Layer 2 — Vital Signs (What does the body show?)
- Non-survivors have lower MAP, higher HR, lower SpO2, and higher RR compared to survivors
- The min values (worst readings over 72h) are more discriminative than means — the worst moment matters more than the average
- Temperature shows fever in some non-survivors but also hypothermia in septic shock patients

### Layer 3 — Laboratory Values (What do the labs reveal?)
- Lactate is the single strongest separator between survivors and non-survivors
- Creatinine, BUN, and bilirubin are all elevated in non-survivors — indicating multi-organ dysfunction
- WBC shows both very high (infection/sepsis) and very low (bone marrow suppression) values in non-survivors

### Layer 4 — Missing Data (What don't we know?)
- Lab values have 30–60% missingness — especially bilirubin and lactate
- This is clinically meaningful — sicker patients often have more tests ordered, but also more missing values due to clinical urgency
- The missing data map helps clinicians understand which features the model relies on less

### Visualisation notebook (5_Data_Visualization.ipynb)

Section 1 — Dataset Overview  
- Outcome distribution (pie), age by outcome (box), gender by outcome (bar)

Section 2 — Distribution Explorer  
- Interactive dropdown: select any numeric feature, plot as histogram/box/violin split by outcome

Section 3 — Vital Signs Range Viewer  
- Min/mean/max ranges per vital, side by side for survivors vs non-survivors

Section 4 — Categorical Breakdown  
- Mortality rate by care unit, admission type, race

Section 5 — Scatter Explorer  
- Any two numeric features, coloured by outcome, with sample size control

Section 6 — Correlation Heatmap  
- Toggle between Vitals, Labs, or All means + mortality

Section 7 — Missing Data Map  
- Horizontal bar chart showing % missingness per feature

Section 8 — Age and LOS Risk Stratification  
- Filter by age range and care unit, see mortality rates within subgroups
